FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about creating your Spayway account and navigating the member workspace.

Create Account →
How do I access the Spayway workspace?

Click Sign In or Register in the top navigation bar to open the dedicated authentication portal, where you can sign in or register with your phone number or email.

Can I register with my international phone number or email?

Yes. The registration page supports both Phone Number (with all global country dialing codes) and Email Address, along with password confirmation, graphic verification code, and invitation code.

Does the workspace work on mobile phones?

Yes. Spayway includes a dedicated mobile layout with a slide-over drawer and a 5-tab bottom navigation bar for Home, Discover, Stake, Team, and Personal Center.
